### A Better Compromise: The "Delayed Reveal" System

You can still preserve the struggle and the "thinking" without depriving yourself of the necessary feedback. Here is a system that gives you the best of both worlds:

- **The Blind Score:** At the end of the 10 questions, give yourself the final score (e.g., 8/10).
- **Reveal the Misses, Not the Answers:** Show exactly _which_ questions you got wrong, but do not highlight the correct option.
- **The Recycling Pool:** Take the questions you missed (or timed out on) and throw them back into your random pool to appear on a future day. This forces you to re-evaluate the analogy from scratch the next time you see it, applying the "training" you want.
- **The "Three Strikes" Rule:** If you miss the exact same question three times across different sessions, the system finally reveals the answer. At that point, your brain has done all the heavy lifting it can, and you need the "aha!" moment to correct the mental block.
