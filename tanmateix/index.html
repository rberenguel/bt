v...

Tanmateix is a fast-paced logic puzzle game where you evaluate whether conclusions logically follow from given premises.

How to Play

You'll be presented with statements (premises) about entities and their relationships. Your task is to determine if the conclusion logically follows from the premises by selecting True or False before time runs out.

Progressive Difficulty

Every 2 correct answers increases the difficulty by adding more paths, more entities, or reducing the time limit. Can you reach the maximum difficulty?

Relationship Types

Questions feature various logical relationships:

  • Linear (28 dimensions): size, speed, temperature, latency, throughput, availability, and more
  • Spatial: directional positioning (north, south, east, west, etc.)
  • Syllogistic: subset and disjoint category reasoning

Example

Premise 1: A is larger than B
Premise 2: B is larger than C
Conclusion: A is larger than C? True

Challenge yourself through 30 questions with increasing complexity!
